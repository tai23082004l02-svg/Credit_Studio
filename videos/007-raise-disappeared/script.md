# Your Raise Disappeared Before Payday

You finally get a raise.

An extra three hundred dollars a month lands in your bank account.

For a moment, life feels easier.

So you upgrade your phone. Thirty dollars more.

You join a nicer gym. Fifty dollars more.

You start ordering delivery more often. Another hundred.

Then come a few subscriptions, a little shopping, and a more expensive weekend.

By the end of the month, the raise is gone.

Each purchase felt small. Together, they gave your entire raise a job before you chose one.

Try this before your next raise arrives.

Decide how much you want to save. Set up an automatic transfer. Then choose what you want to enjoy with the rest.

You worked for that extra money.

Let some of it buy something you cannot put in a shopping cart:

A little more breathing room.
